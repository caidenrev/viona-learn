## Brand & Style

This design system crafts a cozy, emotionally reassuring personal learning and planning environment inspired by Korean stationery journals, pastel sticker sheets, and tactile study desks. The interface transforms routine digital interactions into the comforting feeling of opening a beloved personal agenda decorated with die-cut vinyl stickers, washi tape ribbons, and warm handwritten marginalia.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Sweet & Encouraging:** Interactions act like an affectionate study buddy cheering the user forward with handwritten notes ("Semangat!", "Belajar Yuk!", "Bisa Kok!").
- **Playful & Tactile:** Components evoke real stationery: white-haloed die-cut stickers, lightly pinned memo slips, squishy tactile buttons, and hand-lettered prompts.
- **Gentle Focus:** Avoids clinical corporate minimalism and harsh pure blacks, using warm milky ivory surfaces, soft cocoa ink tones, and gentle pastel washes to prevent visual fatigue.

### Visual Style Hybrid: Kawaii Tactile Stationery
The aesthetic combines skeuomorphic stationery touches with clean, structured digital ergonomics:
- Visual elements sit on die-cut white borders with warm, subtle drop shadows.
- Micro-motifs—tiny sparkles, hearts, stars, bear paws, and ribbon banners—accentuate achievements and section headers without creating friction.
- Organic handwritten typography lends authenticity, making tasks and milestones feel hand-scribed rather than automated.

## Colors

The color palette centers around Japanese and Korean pastel stationery washes, balanced by deep cocoa brown text for accessibility and warmth.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (Sakura Pink - `#FFAAA6`):** The primary emotional accent. Applied to main action buttons, highlighted tabs, ribbon hearts, and milestone badges. Its deep accessible pairing is `#8E4B49`.
- **Secondary (Pastel Lilac - `#C8B6FF`):** Secondary interactive elements, syllabus flags, completed task ribbons, and speech bubble frames. Supported by `#645495` for dark contrasts.
- **Tertiary (Pastel Mint & Sky - `#BEE1E6`):** Supportive containers, progress tracker bars, and helper notes.
- **Neutral (Milk Ivory - `#FAF7F2`):** The ambient canvas background resembling textured off-white planner paper.
- **Cocoa Ink (`#4A3E3D`):** Primary text color replacing pure black with a softer, pencil/gel-pen warm brown tone.
- **Butter Yellow (`#FFF3B0`):** Reserved for sticky notes, highlight stars, and focus badges.
- **Sticker Border Halo (`#FFFFFF` & `#EADFD5`):** Dual-layer outer stroke for cards, badges, and avatars simulating physical sticker cutouts.

## Typography

Typography delivers the aesthetic of a handwritten aesthetic diary or study planner, using carefully calibrated handwriting typefaces that preserve high legibility while exuding charm.

- **Display & Headings (Gaegu):** An expressive, bubbly handwritten Korean-inspired Latin font with organic strokes. Used for greeting titles, cheer banners, section titles, and sticker text.
- **Body & Paragraphs (Patrick Hand):** A clean, friendly marker handwriting style with tall x-height and open counters. It provides high reading comfort for instructions, notes, and study cards without causing eye fatigue.
- **Badges, Labels & Microcopy (Mali):** A cute, rounded cursive handwriting hybrid with structured proportions. Used for interactive buttons, ribbon tags ("BELAJAR YUK!", "TO DO LIST"), checklist tags, and time indicators.
- **Sizing Adaptation:** Because handwritten typefaces appear slightly more compact than geometric grotesques, font sizes are bumped up by 1–2px relative to standard sans-serif baselines to ensure immediate readability on both mobile and desktop viewports.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout arranges items like stationery arranged across a warm student study mat.

### Layout Rhythm
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column grid with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`. Organizes a floating left navigation desk, center activity planner, and right companion memo board.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `margin: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Side panels fold into rounded floating action drawers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column flow with `margin-mobile: 1rem` and `gutter-mobile: 0.75rem`. Cards stack like planner memo sheets.

### Spacing Tokens
- `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) define tight inline icon groupings, badge padding, and sticker tag margins.
- `space-md` (16px) establishes internal spacing for memo pads, list items, and checklist lines.
- `space-lg` (24px) sets standard card padding, creating an airy, comfortable diary feel.
- `space-xl` (36px) separates major modules and roadmap chapters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons sterile blurred drops in favor of physical stationery metaphors: die-cut sticker edges, tape overlays, and squishy 3D buttons.

### Die-Cut Sticker Surface (Cards & Badges)
- Outer rim: 2.5px solid `#FFFFFF` halo rim backed by a delicate 1.5px outline stroke in soft cocoa `#EADFD5`.
- Shadow: `0px 4px 10px rgba(141, 110, 99, 0.08)`, a warm, cocoa-tinted ambient shadow mimicking vinyl stickers placed on paper.

### Pinned Memo & Floating Cards (Interactive Widgets)
- Dual soft shadow: `0px 6px 18px rgba(141, 110, 99, 0.10), 0px 2px 6px rgba(200, 182, 255, 0.12)`.
- Playful rotational accent: Subtle static tilt (`rotate(-1deg)` to `rotate(1deg)`) on memo notes with a translucent washi tape strip at the top.

### Tactile Squishy Button Depth
- Idle state: Supported by a 3px solid bottom border (`border-bottom: 3px solid rgba(141, 110, 99, 0.22)`).
- Active state: Displaces `2px` downward with the bottom border reducing to `1px`, providing a delightful physical press feedback.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy prioritizes pill curves, rounded corners, and soft die-cut silhouettes.

- **Pill Silhouettes (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Applied to primary buttons, ribbon banner tags, status chips, and progress meters.
- **Card Containers (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem - 2rem):** Memo sheets and lesson containers feature generously rounded corners mimicking smooth sticker paper.
- **Speech Bubbles & Ribbon Banners:** Pill shapes with triangular bubble tails or fishtail/swallow-tail ribbon notches on left and right ends.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Squishy Button:** Filled with Sakura Pink (`#FFAAA6`), Deep Cocoa text (`#4A3E3D`), Mali font (semi-bold uppercase). Includes a 3px darker pink/cocoa bottom border edge (`#E89490`) that compresses on click.
- **Secondary Bubble Button:** Lavender background (`#C8B6FF`) with a subtle white gloss highlight on top and soft cocoa text.
- **Outline / Washi Button:** Transparent surface with 2px dashed border in Latte Brown (`#8D6E63`), filled on hover with butter yellow.

### 2. Ribbon Banners & Sticker Badges
- **Ribbon Header:** Curved or swallowtail-ended pastel ribbon carrying encouragement headings ("BELAJAR YUK!", "SEMANGAT!") in Gaegu typography.
- **Mascot Speech Bubbles:** Pill containers with a small pointer tail containing study tips from the bear, bunny, or kitten companions.
- **Sticker Chips:** Rounded white pills with a 2px pastel outline containing an icon (heart, star, paw print) and a handwritten label in Mali.

### 3. Memo Pads & Planner Cards
- **Lined Note Card:** Soft butter yellow (`#FFF3B0`) or crisp white sticker card with horizontal ruled lines (`1px dashed #E2D38B`) and Patrick Hand handwritten entries.
- **To-Do List Container:** White sticker background, top title ("TO DO LIST" in Gaegu), with square or heart outline checkboxes.

### 4. Form Controls & Checklist Items
- **Heart & Star Checkboxes:** Custom rounded squircle or heart checkboxes. Unchecked: 2px soft cocoa outline. Checked: Filled with Sakura Pink or Gold Star with a soft pop bounce.
- **Text Inputs:** Soft Ivory background, 2px solid Lavender border on focus, with placeholder text in light cocoa Patrick Hand font ("Tulis catatanmu di sini...").

### 5. Progress Roadmaps & Meters
- **Pill Progress Bar:** Dual pastel gradient fill (mint to sakura pink) enclosed in a thick pill container, tracked with a cute sliding mascot head icon at the lead position.
- **Milestone Nodes:** Circular die-cut sticker badges linked by dashed pastel connection lines. Completed nodes display a golden star or pink ribbon bow.
